# Google Play Store — Content Rating Questionnaire

Answer these questions when filling out the content rating questionnaire in Google Play Console.

## Violence
- Does your app contain violence? **No**
- Does your app contain depictions of realistic violence? **No**

## Language
- Does your app contain profanity or explicit language? **No**

## Gambling
- Does your app simulate gambling? **No**
- Does your app include real-money gambling? **No**

## Controlled Substances
- Does your app reference alcohol, tobacco, or drugs? **No**

## User Interaction
- Does your app allow users to interact with each other? **Yes**
- Does your app have a chat feature? **Yes**
  - Chat is between accepted friends only
  - Blocked users cannot send messages or challenges

## Location
- Does your app share user location? **No**

## User Generated Content
- Do users create content? **No** (only pre-defined chess moves)

## Digital Purchases
- Does your app offer in-app purchases? **Yes**
  - Premium subscription for unlimited Move Insights
  - Purchases are handled via Creem (Merchant of Record, web-based checkout)

## Expected Rating Result: **Everyone (E)**

The app is a chess game with optional chat between friends.
No violence beyond animated chess piece movements.
No profanity, gambling, drugs, or location sharing.
