Welcome to ChessDuo by Navron — the multiplayer chess app where teammates make simultaneous hidden moves. Think you know chess? Try coordinating with a partner without talking.

═══ HOW IT WORKS ═══
• You and your teammate each pick a move — in secret
• Both moves are revealed at the same time
• Stockfish evaluates both and picks the best one
• If you chose the same move? Bonus sync points
• If you disagreed? The more accurate move wins

It's chess meets teamwork. Trust your partner. Or don't.

═══ GAME MODES ═══

👥 PLAY TOGETHER (2v2) — Create a room, share a code, and team up with a friend against the AI. Real-time via Supabase.

🎮 PLAY OFFLINE — Practice solo with a bot teammate against AI opponents. 6 difficulty levels from Beginner to Grandmaster.

⚡ QUICK MATCH — Auto-find a human teammate and jump into a 2v2 game. No setup, no codes.

⚔️ DUEL — Challenge a friend to 1v1 chess. Standard rules, real-time play.

🔄 4-PLAYER MODE — Two teams of two on the same board. Each team shares a colour and coordinates secretly. Also supports all-human mode for private lobbies.

═══ FEATURES ═══

♟ DUAL-MOVE SYSTEM — See both your move and your teammate's move on the board simultaneously. Green/red highlights show whose move won the accuracy comparison.

📊 PERFORMANCE TRACKING — Per-move accuracy scores, sync rate, and conflict count for every game. Compare your stats in match history.

🔄 MOVE REPLAY — Step through every move of any completed game. See winning moves, shadow moves, and accuracy scores.

💎 MOVE INSIGHTS — Unlock detailed analysis of your best and worst moves (3 free reveals, then premium).

👥 FRIENDS & CHAT — Add friends, send messages, issue challenges. Real-time chat with online presence indicators.

📱 CROSS-PLATFORM — Play on web or Android. One account, all your stats sync.
